Section 4 (Voluntary Adoption): The employer may offer a telecommuting program on a voluntary basis. No employee shall be compelled to telecommute; neither shall an employee be discriminated against for refusing to telecommute.

Section 5 (Equal Treatment): A telecommuting employee shall be given the same treatment as a comparable employee working at the employer's premises with respect to: (a) the right to rest periods, overtime pay, holidays, and leave entitlements under the Labor Code; (b) the same training opportunities and career development path; (c) the same collective bargaining rights as on-site employees; (d) workload, performance standards, and productivity expectations comparable to those of on-site employees.

Section 6 (Data Protection): The employer shall take responsibility for the protection of data processed by a telecommuting employee. The employer must inform the employee of all relevant data privacy laws and the company's data protection policies.

Section 7 (DOLE Implementing Rules): DOLE shall issue rules and regulations providing minimum standards for equipment, work hours, compensation, and the employer's duty to provide or reimburse work tools needed for telecommuting.
