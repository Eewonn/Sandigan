Probationary employment shall not exceed six (6) months from the date the employee started working. The employee shall be informed, at the time of engagement, of the standards under which they will qualify as a regular employee. An employee who is allowed to work after the probationary period shall be considered a regular employee with respect to that activity. Termination during probation requires just cause or failure to meet the disclosed performance standards. A probationary employee wrongfully dismissed may claim illegal dismissal.
