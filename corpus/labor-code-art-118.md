It shall be unlawful for an employer to refuse to pay or reduce the wages and benefits, discharge or in any manner discriminate against any employee who has filed any complaint or instituted any proceeding under this Title or has testified or is about to testify in such proceedings.

An employer who dismisses or demotes a worker in retaliation for filing a complaint with DOLE, the NLRC, or any labor tribunal commits an unfair labor practice. The worker may file a separate complaint for illegal dismissal and for violation of Article 118, and may be entitled to reinstatement with full back wages, moral and exemplary damages, and attorney's fees. Retaliation against a worker who merely announced intent to file a complaint is covered.
