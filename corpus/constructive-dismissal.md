Constructive dismissal occurs when an employer makes continued employment so unreasonable, unlikely, or impossible that the employee is compelled to resign. It includes demotion in rank or pay without valid cause, transfer to a far-away or undesirable position, deliberate exclusion from work, or creation of a hostile work environment. Constructive dismissal is treated as illegal dismissal under Article 294: the employee is entitled to reinstatement, full backwages, and damages even though they technically "resigned."
