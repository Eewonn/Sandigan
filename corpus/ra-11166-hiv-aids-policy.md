Section 16 (Prohibition on Discriminatory Acts): No discrimination in any form shall be committed against any person on the basis of actual, perceived, or suspected HIV status. Prohibited acts in the workplace include: refusing employment, terminating employment, reducing compensation or benefits, and creating a hostile work environment on account of HIV status.

Section 17 (HIV Testing): HIV testing shall NOT be required as a precondition for employment, re-employment, or continued employment. An employer who requires HIV testing as a condition of hiring or continued service commits a prohibited act under this law. HIV test results are strictly confidential and may not be disclosed to an employer without the written informed consent of the person tested.

Section 43 (Penalties): Violations are punishable by imprisonment of not less than six (6) months and one (1) day to not more than twelve (12) years, and/or a fine of not less than ten thousand pesos (₱10,000) to five hundred thousand pesos (₱500,000).

Practical note: If an employer terminates or demotes an employee upon learning (or suspecting) their HIV status, this is illegal termination. The employee may file a complaint at the DOLE or NLRC and may also file a criminal complaint. The Philippine HIV and AIDS Council (PHAC) and the Department of Health (DOH) can provide assistance.
