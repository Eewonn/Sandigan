Nothing in this Book shall be construed to eliminate or in any way diminish supplements, or other employee benefits being enjoyed at the time of promulgation of this Code.

Jurisprudence (Supreme Court) extends this to benefits that have ripened into company practice: if an employer has consistently and voluntarily granted a benefit (e.g., annual clothing allowance, transportation subsidy, productivity bonus, meal allowance) over a long period without conditions, such benefit becomes a demandable right and cannot be unilaterally reduced or eliminated without violating Article 100. A one-time, conditional, or declared-ex-gratia grant generally does not ripen into enforceable practice.
