The State shall ensure the payment of a minimum wage to employees. Regional Tripartite Wages and Productivity Boards (RTWPBs) determine and fix minimum wage rates applicable in their respective regions, provinces, or industries. Paying below the prevailing regional minimum wage is unlawful.
