A female employee who undergoes surgery caused by a gynecological disorder is entitled to a special leave benefit of up to two (2) months with full pay based on her gross monthly compensation. To qualify, she must have rendered at least six (6) months of continuous aggregate employment service for the last twelve months prior to the surgery. This leave is in addition to any other existing leave entitlements and cannot be denied by the employer.
