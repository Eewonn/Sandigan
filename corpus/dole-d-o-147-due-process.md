Procedural due process in dismissal for just cause requires the twin-notice rule: (1) a written notice specifying the ground for termination and giving the employee a reasonable opportunity to explain his side; and (2) a written notice of termination indicating that upon due consideration of all the circumstances, grounds have been established to justify dismissal. A hearing or conference is also required where the employee is given the opportunity to respond, present evidence, or rebut evidence presented against him.
