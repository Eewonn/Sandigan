Section 5 (Equal Opportunity for Employment): No disabled person shall be denied access to opportunities for suitable employment. A qualified disabled employee shall be subject to the same terms and conditions of employment and the same compensation, privileges, benefits, fringe benefits, incentives, or allowances as a qualified able-bodied person.

Section 8 (Prohibition on Discrimination): No entity, whether public or private, shall discriminate against a qualified disabled person by reason of disability in regard to job application procedures, the hiring, promotion, or discharge of employees, employee compensation, job training, and other terms, conditions, and privileges of employment.

Section 32 (Tax Incentives): Private entities that employ disabled persons who meet the required skills or qualifications, either as regular employee, apprentice or learner, shall be entitled to an additional deduction from their gross income, equivalent to twenty-five percent (25%) of the total amount paid as salaries and wages to disabled persons.

RA 10524 (2013 Amendment): Private corporations with more than one hundred (100) employees are encouraged to reserve at least one percent (1%) of all positions for PWDs. Government agencies and instrumentalities shall reserve at least one percent (1%) of all their positions for PWDs.

Practical note: "Disability" includes physical, mental, and psychosocial impairments. An employer cannot terminate a PWD employee solely because of their disability. Employers are expected to provide reasonable accommodation (e.g., accessible workstations) unless it would impose an undue burden.
