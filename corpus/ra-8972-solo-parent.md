A solo parent employee who has rendered at least one (1) year of service is entitled to a parental leave of seven (7) working days per year, with pay, in addition to other leave privileges. Solo parents must present a valid Solo Parent ID issued by the DSWD to their employer. The leave benefit cannot be converted to cash. A solo parent is defined as any individual who falls within the definition of RA 8972 (e.g., single parent due to death of spouse, legal separation, unmarried mother/father, etc.).
