Coverage in the SSS shall be compulsory upon all employees including kasambahays or domestic workers not over sixty (60) years of age and their employers. The employer shall deduct the monthly contributions from the employee's compensation and remit the same, together with the employer's share, to the SSS. Failure to remit contributions is punishable.
