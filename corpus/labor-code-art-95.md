Every employee who has rendered at least one (1) year of service shall be entitled to a yearly service incentive leave of five (5) days with pay. This provision shall not apply to those already enjoying the benefit, those enjoying vacation leave with pay of at least five days, and those employed in establishments regularly employing less than ten employees.
