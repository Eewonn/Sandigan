Step 1 — SEnA (Single Entry Approach): Before filing a formal NLRC case, most labor disputes must first go through SEnA at the nearest DOLE field office. A mediator (SEADO) facilitates settlement within 30 days. This is FREE. If unresolved, the worker receives a referral (SEnA referral) to file at NLRC.

Step 2 — NLRC Complaint: File a verified complaint (NLRC Form 1) at the Regional Arbitration Branch (RAB) that has jurisdiction over the workplace. Simple money claims up to PHP 5,000 may be filed directly at the DOLE Regional or Field Office without going to NLRC.

Step 3 — Documents to gather: (a) employment contract or company ID; (b) payslips, pay stubs, or any proof of wages received; (c) notice of dismissal or constructive dismissal evidence; (d) any written demand letter and company reply; (e) SSS/PhilHealth/PAGIBIG contribution records (print from their portals); (f) text messages, emails, or other communications showing violations.

Step 4 — Fees: Filing at NLRC is FREE for workers with money claims. There is no docketing fee for employees filing for illegal dismissal or unpaid wages.

Step 5 — Prescription periods (filing deadline): Illegal dismissal — 4 years from dismissal date. Money claims (unpaid wages, overtime, holiday pay, 13th month, etc.) — 3 years from when each claim became due. File as soon as possible; prescription cannot be waived by the employer.
