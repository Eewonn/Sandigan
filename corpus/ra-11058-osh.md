Section 6 (Worker Rights): Every worker shall have the right to: (a) be informed by the employer about all types of hazardous and harmful exposures in the workplace; (b) receive safety instruction and training at no cost; (c) report accidents, dangerous occurrences, and hazards to the employer or DOLE; (d) refuse unsafe work without loss of pay or employment benefits where there is an imminent danger of death or serious physical harm, provided the worker immediately reports this to DOLE.

Section 7 (Employer Obligations): The employer shall: provide a safe workplace free from hazardous conditions that cause or are likely to cause death, illness, or physical harm; comply with DOLE OSH standards; provide adequate personal protective equipment (PPE) at no cost to workers; conduct regular workplace OSH programs; and immediately report work-related accidents, dangerous occurrences, and occupational illnesses to DOLE.

Section 9 (DOLE Enforcement): DOLE may issue a Work Stoppage Order (WSO) where imminent danger is found. Employers who willfully violate OSH standards causing death or serious injury face criminal liability. Fines of up to PHP 100,000 per day of violation may be imposed until the hazard is corrected.
