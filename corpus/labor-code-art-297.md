An employer may terminate an employment for any of the following causes: (a) Serious misconduct or willful disobedience by the employee of the lawful orders of his employer; (b) Gross and habitual neglect by the employee of his duties; (c) Fraud or willful breach of the trust reposed by the employer; (d) Commission of a crime or offense against the person of the employer; and (e) Other analogous causes.
