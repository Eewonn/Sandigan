In the absence of a retirement plan or CBA, an employee in the private sector who retires at age 60 (optional) to 65 (compulsory) with at least five (5) years of service is entitled to a retirement pay equivalent to at least one-half (1/2) month salary per year of service. One-half month salary is computed as 22.5 days: 15 days basic pay + 5 days proportionate service incentive leave + 1/12 of the 13th-month pay. A fraction of at least six months is counted as one whole year.
