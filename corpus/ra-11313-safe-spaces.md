The employer shall be responsible for the prevention and deterrence of acts of gender-based sexual harassment in the workplace, including disseminating the policy, providing training, creating a committee on decorum and investigation (CODI), and providing an independent, internal mechanism to receive complaints. Employers who fail to act on reported incidents may be held liable.
