Section 43 (Battered Woman Leave): A victim-employee who is a victim of violence under RA 9262 shall be entitled to a paid leave of up to ten (10) days in addition to other paid leaves under the Labor Code, existing laws, and company policies. This leave is extendible when the necessity arises as specified in a protection order issued by the barangay or court.

Who qualifies: Women who are victims of physical, sexual, psychological, or economic abuse by a spouse, former spouse, or any person with whom they have or had a romantic or sexual relationship, or with whom they share a common child.

How to avail: The employee must present a Barangay Protection Order (BPO), Temporary Protection Order (TPO), or Permanent Protection Order (PPO) issued by the appropriate authority; or a police report, medical certificate, or any similar document showing that violence occurred.

Employer obligation: The employer must grant the leave immediately upon presentation of supporting documentation. The employer may not compel the employee to disclose specific details of the violence as a condition of granting the leave.

Practical note: If your employer denies this leave or threatens to discipline you for availing it, file a complaint at the DOLE Regional Office. The leave is non-commutative — unused days cannot be converted to cash.
