Membership in the Home Development Mutual Fund (Pag-IBIG / HDMF) is mandatory for all private sector employees who are covered by the SSS. The employer must deduct the employee's monthly contribution from their salary and add the employer counterpart share, remitting both to Pag-IBIG. Non-remittance by the employer is punishable by law. Members may use accumulated contributions as collateral for housing loans, multi-purpose loans, and calamity loans. Members can verify contributions at the Pag-IBIG virtual Pag-IBIG portal.
