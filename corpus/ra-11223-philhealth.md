All Filipinos are automatically covered by the National Health Insurance Program administered by PhilHealth. For employed workers, the employer must deduct the employee's monthly premium contribution from their compensation and remit both the employer and employee shares to PhilHealth on time. Failure to remit is an offense punishable by fine and/or imprisonment. Employees can check their posted contributions via the PhilHealth member portal (www.philhealth.gov.ph). Non-remittance deprives the employee of hospital benefits.
