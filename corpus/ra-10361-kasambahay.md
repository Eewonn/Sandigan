Domestic workers (kasambahay) — including household helpers, yaya, cook, gardener, and laundry person — are entitled to: (1) the minimum wage applicable in the National Capital Region or the region where they work; (2) one rest day per week; (3) 5 days annual service incentive leave; (4) 13th month pay; (5) mandatory SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG coverage (employer registers and pays contributions); (6) free board, lodging, and medical attendance if staying in the employer's residence; and (7) the right to communicate with family. Employers who violate these rights may be sanctioned. Kasambahay may file complaints with the DOLE.
