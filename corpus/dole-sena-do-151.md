The Single Entry Approach (SEnA) is a mandatory 30-day conciliation-mediation proceeding before a formal labor case can be filed. Any aggrieved worker may file a Request for Assistance (RFA) at the nearest DOLE Regional/Provincial/Field Office or NLRC — filing is free. A desk officer (SEADO) will summon both parties and attempt to reach a settlement within 30 days. If unresolved, the matter is referred to the appropriate body: the NLRC for money claims and illegal dismissal, the DOLE Regional Director for labor-standard violations (e.g., underpayment). Parties may appear without a lawyer.
